# Spheres

The Everything Ecosystem is a schema (EE-002). A **sphere** is the unit of an instance: one thing a builder builds, run by the same layers (EE-008). The name comes from Kuyper's sphere sovereignty, in which each sphere governs itself and the borders between spheres are kept. "Domain" was rejected because it already means a subject area here (domain design, MAD's `domain` field).

Personal life is out of scope (EE-009). Personal knowledge management will be a separate system, built on its own; once both exist, it can join an instance through links like any other sphere.

Research and prior art: the Claude Doc "Domains: name, kinds and prior art" (https://claude.ai/artifact/7KdsXr2EmrcnB6ahtvxzhH). Its personal-root sections are superseded by EE-009.

## Rules

1. **Two kinds.** A sphere is a `venture` or a `client`. Top-level spheres are ventures.
2. **Nesting.** A venture can hold ventures (a brand house and its lines) and clients. A client always sits inside the venture that serves it. This follows Beer's Viable System Model: every viable system contains, and is contained in, a viable system.
3. **Stages, not kinds.** Hobby, venture and company are stages of one venture (EE-004). A company needs a `legal_form`. Changing stage never moves folders; grouping folders such as `ventures/companies/` are for browsing only.
4. **Same layers everywhere.** Purpose, Knowledge, Operations, Execution and Evidence (plus Governance and Interface) exist in every sphere, at the depth it needs. A layer a sphere doesn't use yet is recorded as `false`.
5. **Three pillars in every venture.** Product, brand and business, from the venture template (`governance/company-template/pillars/`), at every stage, legal entity or not (EE-010). Governance starts at the company stage.
6. **A profile beside the record.** `sphere.json` holds the machine fields; the profile it names holds the narrative and changelog (EE-008).
7. **One-way links.** A link either reads another sphere or shares named things with it, with a border that is closed, filtered or open. This follows Clark's work/family border theory.
8. **Spheres open and close.** Each has a status and dates. A client engagement ends; a venture can be paused or archived.

## Kinds

| Kind | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| venture | self or shared | Anything a builder undertakes. `stage`: hobby (for its own sake), venture (risk and intent, not yet a company) or company (formalized; needs `legal_form`: llc, s-corp, c-corp, sole-prop, partnership, nonprofit) |
| client | shared | Only inside the venture that serves it (`<venture>/clients/<client>/`) |

## Files

- `schema/workspace.schema.json`: the `workspace.json` that declares an instance, its schema version and where to find spheres.
- `schema/sphere.schema.json`: one `sphere.json` at the root of each sphere.
- `schema/check-spheres.mjs`: the rules across spheres (unique ids, parents exist, only ventures at the top, spheres sit inside ventures, no loops, stage and legal form where they belong, links point at known spheres).
- `schema/scaffold/defaults.json` and `schema/init-sphere.mjs`: create an empty sphere with its starting layers, a profile, and for a venture the three pillars.
- `schema/examples/spheres/`: a company with a product line and a client, and a hobby.
