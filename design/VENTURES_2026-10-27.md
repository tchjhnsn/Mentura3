# Ventures

The Everything Ecosystem is a schema (EE-002). A **venture** is its unit: anything a builder undertakes, from a hobby to a company (EE-011). Personal life is out of scope (EE-009); a personal system will be built on its own and joined later through links.

Research behind these choices:
- Venture classification (industry, market and what sits beside them): https://claude.ai/artifact/Bb3N3V2TjK9AnyrUAzy4AA
- Ontology (the nouns at every level, the phases, references): https://claude.ai/artifact/6LcDgsc8SA9ybY5ddTfsMn
- Sources: `purpose/references/references.json`. Nouns: `docs/ontology.md`.

## Rules

1. **One unit.** Everything a builder undertakes is a venture. Ventures can hold ventures (a brand house and its lines), following Beer's Viable System Model.
2. **Phase, not stage** (EE-012). A venture is in one of five phases, each with a gate for leaving it:

   | Phase | Question | Gate to leave it |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Discovery | Does a real problem exist, and who has it? | Problem-solution fit |
   | Validation | Will people adopt this solution and keep using it? | Product-market fit: retention holds |
   | Beachhead | Can we win one segment repeatably, at a profitable cost? | Repeatability |
   | Scale | How fast can we take the mass market? | Market leadership with healthy margins |
   | Platform | What will others build on, so the venture can't be displaced? | Others build on it |

3. **Formalization and intent are separate fields.** Whether a venture is formalized is its `form.legal_form`. Why it exists is its `ambition`: hobby (in the IRS sense), survival, lifestyle, managed growth or aggressive growth. Ambition says how far through the phases it means to go; a hobby can stay in Discovery for good.
4. **The core product comes first.** `venture.json` holds the core product (promise, promised to, must not, competence). It is required once a venture leaves Discovery, because no product can be ranked before it exists.
5. **What is true per product lives on the product.** Each product is a card in `products/`: market, job, technology, offering, business model, and the products it flows to. Product Anatomy judges each against the core and assigns its portfolio role in each phase. `venture.json` keeps only what is true of the whole venture: phase, ambition, form, industry and core.
6. **Named parties are account records.** An account lives at `accounts/<id>/account.json` inside the venture that serves it, with a relationship (client, customer, user, member, partner); it has no phase or core. (It was the client record, retired by EE-021 because clients are not universal.)
7. **Same layers everywhere, three pillars in every venture.** Purpose, Knowledge, Operations, Execution and Evidence (plus Governance and Interface) exist in every venture at the depth it needs; a layer not used yet is `false`. Starting layers come from the phase. Governance switches on when the venture is formalized: a legal form other than none, or other members. Every venture gets the product, brand and business pillars from the venture template (EE-010).
8. **One-way links.** A link either reads another venture or shares named things with it, with a border that is closed, filtered or open (Clark's border theory).
9. **Opinionated, with references** (EE-014). The system names one default for each choice and records the sources and alternatives behind it in the layer's references.

## Files

- `schema/workspace.schema.json`: the `workspace.json` that declares an instance and where its ventures are.
- `schema/venture.schema.json`, `schema/product.schema.json`, `schema/account.schema.json`: one venture, its product cards, its account records (EE-021).
- `schema/check-ventures.mjs`: rules across ventures (unique ids, parents exist, no loops, links resolve, product and account references stay inside their venture; warns when a formalized venture has no governance layer).
- `schema/init-venture.mjs` and `schema/scaffold/defaults.json`: create a venture with the layers its phase starts with, `products/`, `accounts/`, the pillars and a venture profile.
- `schema/examples/workspace/`: a company in Beachhead with two products, an account and a product line in Validation, and a hobby in Discovery.
- `purpose/frameworks/product-anatomy/`: the Product Anatomy runner reads a venture folder directly.
