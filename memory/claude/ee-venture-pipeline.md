Torian agreed 2026-10-28 21:18Z to this pipeline, framed by what the founder does, not by the files produced. He rejected the sitemap as a stage: it is one record inside the marketing package.

1. Capture
2. Understand
3. Route
4. Decide
5. Write
6. Confirm
7. Package

His points, in his framing:
- **Understand.** Structure is first visualized here. Mentura makes sure it understood the ideas. The founder confirms the capture and corrects that understanding before anything is routed, which makes it a confirm gate like stage 6.
- **Decide** is one of the first real gates the user experiences.
- **Route** is where the venture structure takes shape and the founder first glimpses the package. The payoff from stage 3 to stage 7 is much longer than from 1 to 2 or 2 to 3. So stage 3 needs strong visual support and reward feedback, with progress toward 100% of a package.
- **The package gates automation.** It sits between idea and automation or operations. MAD is the operations framework and harness, and automation is one of its tiers: agents running calls and campaigns, pitch decks and the like. No automation runs until the venture package is complete.
- **The venture package** holds versions of itself and the venture's phase. It is the source of truth for the business side; engineering may have its own. It is maintained over time, likely in git, and built on continuously. For example, a discovery docket item like "get customer feedback" has its results stored in the package, and the step is checked off. The venture moves through all phases this way.

**How to apply:** treat the package as a living, versioned business source of truth, not a final export. Put progress feedback at Route.
