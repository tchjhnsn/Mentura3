Torian, 2026-10-28 21:35Z, about how packages are split:
- How packages are split, and which gates apply, depends on the governance model the founder chooses.
- Start with very small teams and solo founders. Do not model every organizational structure; corporate enterprises are very hard to model.
- That is why the framework is opinionated: it assumes one governance model. The founding team is also the engineering and product management team, and the founders do everything in the business.
- The current iteration is hyper-specific to ventures in the first two phases, Discovery and Validation.

**How to apply:** state the assumed governance model explicitly in packages.json. Mark packages for other structures or later phases as not modeled yet rather than deleting them. Don't design for enterprises or for separate engineering teams. Related: [[ee-venture-pipeline]], [[ee-venture-package]].
