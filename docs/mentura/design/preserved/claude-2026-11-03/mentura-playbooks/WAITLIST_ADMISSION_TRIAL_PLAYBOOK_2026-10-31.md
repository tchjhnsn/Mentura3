# Waitlist admission trial (Mentura)

Draft for Torian to edit. This comes after the discovery interview, never in the same session (decided 2026-10-31 07:56Z). It is how a founder gets off the waitlist and into a cohort.

## What it decides

1. Whether to admit the founder to the next cohort.
2. The founder's and the venture's strengths and weaknesses (these feed the founder's blind-spot module, privately).
3. Which package and engagement to offer: deliverables and compensation (equity, partnership or direct payment).

## Before the session

- Tell the founder plainly that this session is an assessment, what it decides, and how their records will be used. The rubric can stay private; the fact of assessment cannot.
- Ask them for one real problem in their venture right now.
- Choose one to three Naive modules aimed at that problem or their likely weak spot (marketing, for example). Not the whole of Naive: one idea per prototype.
- Write your prediction first, in the interview record (`schema/validation-interview.schema.json`, `prediction`): what they will reach without Naive, and what with it.

## The session

1. **Without Naive (10 minutes).** They work the problem as they normally would. Watch the activities and what they reach.
2. **With Naive (25 minutes).** Give them the chosen modules. Watch, don't steer. Note where they get stuck, what they skip and what they reach.
3. **Debrief (10 minutes).** What changed for them? What would they do next? Do they want to keep going?

## The rubric (private)

Score on evidence seen in the session, not on how well they talk:

- **Outcomes reached** against your prediction, with Naive and without it.
- **Coachability:** did they use the modules, or route around them?
- **Venture fit:** can this venture bootstrap Discovery and Validation (low or medium capital needed)?
- **Founder fit:** the strengths and weaknesses shown, and whether Mentura's packages cover the weaknesses.
- **Capacity:** whether there is room in the cohort, given your mentoring hours.

## After

- Write the interview record, including the follow-up that checks your prediction.
- Admission is a gate: no "admitted" without the interview record and its evidence.
- If admitted: offer the package as an engagement record (deliverables and compensation). Equity needs counsel before anything is signed.
- If not admitted yet: tell them what would change the answer, and keep giving them the waitlist offerings.
