Current model (Torian, 2026-10-27, schema 0.2.0, EE decisions EE-008 to EE-010):
- Unit is the "sphere" (chosen over "domain": it reads plainly, and "domain" collides with domain design and MAD). sphere.json = machine fields; a profile.md beside it is the human-readable companion.
- Kinds: venture and client only. Venture stage hobby | venture | company (company needs legal_form). Ventures can nest (brand house + lines). Clients exist only inside the venture that serves them. Grouping folders like ventures/companies/ are visual only.
- Personal life / PKM is OUT of scope (EE-009 supersedes EE-003, which had a personal root, household/employer/community/learning kinds, values and purpose weights). Torian will build the personal system separately; the two join later via links.
- Company template stays as the venture template: the three pillars (product, brand, business) go into every venture at every stage, legal entity or not (EE-010). Governance starts at the company stage.
- Focus is the EE schema only, independent of Torian's own workspace (he said ignore his instance and ignore Polity for now).

**Why:** Torian wants EE to be about what a builder builds; the personal system has different needs (values, axiology) and is designed on its own.
**How to apply:** don't reintroduce personal kinds or values into the EE schema; cite EE-NNN ids in EE files, not vault G-ids.
