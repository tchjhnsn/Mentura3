Torian 2026-10-31 11:51Z: "we do not want the AI to speak for a human. That is a very bad alignment flaw. That is one of our must never do requirements." AI can never be a mentor (lived experience is required, by definition); at best it is a coach. Even though the platform is called Mentura, the agents are not the mentors; the mentors are humans, a defined services role in the business.

**Why:** impersonating or ventriloquizing a person is an alignment flaw; mentorship rests on a human's lived experience and the sacred trust.

**How to apply:** never design a skill/agent that speaks in a named person's voice or "on behalf of" a mentor (rejected my earlier "Athena pattern" proposal). Agent skills are coaching instruments; the human mentor mentors in person. Add as a must-never requirement wherever requirements are listed.
